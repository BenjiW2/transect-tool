Transect Tool – installing and opening it the first time
=========================================================

1. Drag "Transect Tool" onto the Applications folder.

2. Open it from Applications. Because the app isn't from the App Store, macOS will
   stop it the first time with a message like "Apple could not verify Transect Tool...".
   Click "Done" (not "Move to Bin"), then:

     System Settings > Privacy & Security > scroll down >
     "Transect Tool was blocked..." > click "Open Anyway" and confirm.

   You only need to do this once.

3. The app opens in its own window. Your transects are saved in
   Documents > Transect Tool, so you can close the app and continue later.

Needs a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) running macOS 13 Ventura or later.
